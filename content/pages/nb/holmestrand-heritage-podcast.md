This page is a special tribute to the descendants of emigrants from Holmestrand—a small Norwegian town with a rich history of courage, exploration, and new beginnings. Here, we invite you to connect with your roots through a series of English-language podcasts. These stories dive into the lives of those who left Holmestrand to build new futures across the globe, as well as the legacy they passed down to generations like yours. Whether you’re tracing your family history or simply curious about the emigrant experience, we hope these podcasts will inspire, inform, and bring you closer to the past.
