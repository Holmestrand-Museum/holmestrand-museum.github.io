On the stormy night of **October 12, 1884**, the peaceful town of **Holmestrand, Norway**, was suddenly engulfed in flames. What began as a small fire quickly turned into a raging inferno, consuming **66 buildings** and reducing nearly half the town to ashes. Amid the chaos, townspeople fled, carrying what little they could—some escaping through burning streets, others desperately trying to save loved ones and valuables.

How did this disaster unfold? Could it have been prevented? And how did Holmestrand rise from the ashes?
