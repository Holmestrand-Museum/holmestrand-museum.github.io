Welcome to the **Holmestrand Heritage Podcast**, a series dedicated to bringing the history of Holmestrand, Norway, to life. This podcast is based on *Holmestrand og Omegn*, a historical work by Dr. Gunnar Graarud, and aims to make the town’s past accessible to a global audience, particularly American descendants of Norwegian emigrants.

The episodes are **AI-generated**, using advanced transcription and translation of Dr. Graarud’s original text. While we strive for accuracy, some nuances may be adapted for clarity and storytelling.

Join us on a journey through time, from the town’s earliest days to its growth as a thriving trade center. Through the lives of fishermen, merchants, and settlers, we’ll uncover the rich heritage of Holmestrand—one story at a time.

The original source is available online at the national libary (nb.no). It has no copyright restrictions.
